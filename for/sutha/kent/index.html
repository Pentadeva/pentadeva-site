Ahead of our sit-down

A short read for the management team, so you know why I’m coming and what I’d like to learn.

Why I’d like to meet

I’m Shajan, the founder of Pentadeva. Before this I was an engineering officer in the Royal Navy, where keeping complex systems inspection-ready every single day was the job.

I’m building Pentadeva to take the back-office weight off independent care homes: the rotas, training records, supervisions, audits and inspection evidence that land on managers’ desks and spill into their evenings. Pentadeva is new, and I’m being upfront about that. Before I go further, I want to learn from people who run homes well, and that’s why your time means a lot to me.

The sit-down isn’t a sales pitch. I’m coming to listen and learn how your home really runs, and to find out whether there’s anything I could genuinely help with.

What the sit-down looks like

About 45 minutes, at whichever branch suits you. Nothing to prepare, nothing to show me.

15 minutes: I listen

How a normal week really runs, where the admin goes, and what always ends up in the evenings.

15 minutes: a quick health check

Ten honest questions on rotas, training, audits and CQC evidence, scored together. You keep the sheet.

15 minutes: your thoughts

What you’d want from help like this, if anything, and what I’ve missed. Your feedback shapes what I build.

What I’m building

A service that takes four kinds of work off a manager’s desk:

Rotas and cover

Built and kept up to date, with gaps flagged early so agency costs don’t creep up.

Training and supervisions

Every expiry tracked and chased before it lapses. Supervisions scheduled, recorded and filed.

Audits and actions

Audits run on schedule, with every action chased to closure, which is what CQC’s good-governance standard asks for.

Ready for the new CQC framework

CQC is moving from its 34 quality statements back to key lines of enquiry around the end of 2026. Evidence filed as you go means you’re ready either way.
