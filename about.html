The Founder
Former Royal Navy Weapons Engineering Officer

The short version

I served in the Royal Navy as a Weapons Engineering Officer — a job where safety-critical systems must be inspection-ready every single day, because the inspection that matters can come at any time. The discipline is simple to describe and hard to keep: records current, evidence filed, nothing reconstructed after the fact.

That is precisely the discipline a care home needs for CQC — and precisely what a one-to-five-home owner has no time to run while also covering shifts, managing staff and running a building. So that's the job I do now.

Before founding Pentadeva I also worked in recruitment at Hays, the UK’s largest recruiter — which is why staff files, safe-recruitment compliance and vacancy support are part of the service rather than an add-on.

Why care homes

When my grandfather in Sri Lanka needed care, a well-run care service is what let our family look after him from across the world. I know what these services mean to families when they run well — and how much invisible paperwork it takes to run one.

I grew up in Walthamstow and Ilford, and I still live in Ilford. The service area — East London and Essex — isn't a marketing decision; it's the distance I can actually drive to be in your building when you need me.

The company

Pentadeva Systems Ltd is a back-office support service for independent care homes with one to five homes in East London and Essex. Registered in England & Wales, company no. 12721020. The name comes from "five angels" — and the five founding homes the service is being built around.

Check the company record, email me anything — in this trade you should verify the people you let into your building, and I'd worry about any owner who didn't.
