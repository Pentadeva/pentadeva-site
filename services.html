What we do, and what it costs

One service, one published price per home. No demo, no sales funnel, no "book a call to find out". The work below is delivered in person, by the founder, in your building.

Care home admin & back-office support

The standing load that eats an owner's week — taken over and run to a monthly rhythm:

  • Rotas built and maintained, cover gaps flagged early
  • Training matrix tracked — expiries chased before they lapse, not after
  • Supervision and appraisal schedules kept current, with records filed
  • Admin inbox triage: supplier emails, CQC correspondence, local-authority chasers
  • Policy review dates tracked and documents kept audit-ready

CQC inspection preparation

Around one in three care homes is currently rated below Good, and Well-led is the weakest-rated key question nationally — almost always an evidence problem rather than a care problem. We run inspection readiness as a permanent state, not a panic:

  • Evidence filed continuously against the CQC quality statements
  • Mock inspection walk-throughs before the real one
  • Action plans written, tracked and closed — with a paper trail
  • Your team prepared for what inspectors actually ask

Compliance & audit support

The monthly and quarterly audit cycle a small home rarely has time to run properly:

  • Medication, care-plan, infection-control and premises audit schedules run on time
  • Findings logged with owners and deadlines — and chased to closure
  • Regulation 17 (good governance) oversight records kept inspection-ready

Recruitment & staff-file compliance

Built on recruitment-industry experience at Hays, the UK’s largest recruiter:

  • Staff files kept complete against Schedule 3 safe-recruitment requirements
  • DBS, references and right-to-work tracked with renewal dates
  • Vacancy support: advert drafting, candidate screening, interview scheduling

Questions owners actually ask

Who is this for?

Owner-operators of independent care homes — one to five homes — in East London and Essex. We're built for small independents, not chains. If you run twenty homes with a head office, we're not your fit, and we'll say so.

Is this software we have to learn?

No. We take over the work itself. Any tools we use, we set up and operate for you, in your building. Your staff never learn a new system alone.

We already use care software — does that matter?

Not at all. Care-records systems hold your daily care notes; we run the back office around them — rotas, training, audits, evidence, correspondence. We work with whatever you already have.

What happens in the first month?

We visit, map how the home actually runs, and take over the admin load piece by piece — in person, at your pace. By the end of month one you should already be chasing nothing.

Why is the price published?

Because the alternative — "book a demo to find out" — wastes your time and ours. One price per home, flat. It never changes mid-relationship, and the founding rate is locked in writing.
